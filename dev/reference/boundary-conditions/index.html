Boundary conditions

The boundary conditions block groups conditions by type. Each type key maps to a list of entries:

boundary conditions:
  Dirichlet:
    - node set: nsx-
      component: x
      function: "0.0"
    - node set: nsx-
      component: y
      function: "0.0"
  Neumann:
    - side set: ssz+
      component: z
      function: "1.0e+09 * t"

The block is optional; a model with no boundary conditions has none. This page covers the single-domain condition types. The Schwarz coupling condition types (Schwarz overlap, Schwarz DN nonoverlap, Schwarz RR nonoverlap, Schwarz contact) are documented under Multidomain and Schwarz coupling.

Conditions are applied in a fixed order that does not depend on the order of the type keys in the file: all other types first, in alphabetical order of the type name, and the prescribed types (Dirichlet, OpInf Dirichlet) last. Entries of one type keep their order in the list. A node that belongs to more than one condition, such as an interface edge node that also lies on a symmetry or clamped face, therefore ends up with its prescribed value.

The component value is always one of x, y, or z. The function value is a function expression in t, x, y, z. Geometry is selected by a node set or side set name from the Exodus mesh, as noted per type.

Dirichlet

Prescribes a displacement component. Velocity and acceleration follow by automatic time differentiation (see Function expressions).

node set or side setone requiredgeometry the condition acts on
componentyesx, y, or z
functionyesprescribed displacement
Dirichlet:
  - node set: nsz+
    component: z
    function: "0.01 * t"

Neumann pressure

Applies a scalar follower pressure normal to a side set. There is no component — the direction is the surface normal.

KeyRequiredMeaning
side setyessurface the pressure acts on
functionyesscalar pressure (positive acts along the outward normal)
Neumann pressure:
  - side set: interior_ss_bottom
    function: "-937.5e3 * 1500 * (0.5 - 0.5 * cos(pi * t / 1500.0))"

Robin

A mixed condition traction + (robin parameter)·displacement = function on a side-set component.

KeyRequiredMeaning
side setyessurface the condition acts on
componentyesx, y, or z
functionyesright-hand side
robin parameteryescoefficient on the displacement term (nonzero)

Surface

An inclined-support (roller) condition: the constrained nodes are held on the level surface g(x, y, z) = 0 given by function, free to slide within it. The constraint direction is the surface gradient ∇g, computed automatically; a constant function (no gradient) is rejected. A node in the side sets of two Surface conditions slides along their intersection, and a node in three is fixed at their common point; surfaces that meet tangentially at a node are rejected. Under exact enforcement the descent direction is projected onto the tangent space and every node is returned to its surfaces by a closest-point projection, which requires the steepest descent solver; penalty enforcement adds the force of the penalty ½ κ g² and works with every solver. This is the condition that keeps the boundary nodes of mesh smoothing on curved surfaces, and the adaptivity loop places the nodes that its splits add on the boundary on the same surfaces.

KeyRequiredDefaultMeaning
side setyes—surface whose nodes are constrained
functionyes—level-set expression g in t, x, y, z
enforcementnoexactexact or penalty
penaltyno1.0e6penalty coefficient (positive; used when enforcement: penalty)

Periodic

Periodic conditions on a box cell, the boundary conditions of a representative volume element. Each pair of node sets lists two opposite faces of the box, the second the image of the first under a translation along the positive x, y or z axis. For every node X⁻ of the first set and its image X⁺ on the second,

u(X⁺) − u(X⁻) = (F̄ − I)(X⁺ − X⁻),

where F̄ is the macroscopic deformation gradient, which for a periodic field is the volume average of F over the cell. Every component F̄ᵢⱼ whose column j is the axis of a pair is controlled in one of two ways: prescribed as a function of t under deformation gradient, or free under stress, with the same component of P̄ prescribed instead. P̄ is the volume average of the first Piola–Kirchhoff stress, the work conjugate of F̄. A component listed in neither map is held at its value in the identity.

KeyRequiredDefaultMeaning
node set pairsyes—one to three pairs [first, second] of node sets on opposite faces
deformation gradientnoidentityprescribed components of F̄, as functions of t
stressnononefree components of F̄, with the prescribed components of P̄ as functions of t

Components are named by row and column: xy is F̄ₓᵧ = ∂x/∂Y, and the names are xx, xy, xz, yx, yy, yz, zx, zy and zz. A component may appear in only one of the two maps. The rigid rotation has to stay fixed, so of xy and yx (and of xz and zx, and of yz and zy) at most one may be under stress. With one or two pairs, a plane that contains an axis that is not periodic has one component of F̄, or none: a cell periodic along x and y has zx but not xz, and a cell periodic along x only has no component in the y–z plane. The rotation in such a plane is held when its component is prescribed; otherwise another condition has to hold it, such as a Dirichlet condition on a node away from the origin, and the run aborts if none does. A cell periodic along x only, with free lateral faces, needs one for the rotation about x, for example on component z of the corner node set nsx-y+z-, which a homogeneous deformation leaves at zero.

Uniaxial stress along x prescribes F̄ₓₓ and leaves every other component of P̄ at zero. The lower shears yx, zx and zy stay at zero and fix the rotation. The free upper shears let an anisotropic cell shear under the axial load, and the average stress is then uniaxial:

Periodic:
  - node set pairs: [[nsx-, nsx+], [nsy-, nsy+], [nsz-, nsz+]]
    deformation gradient:
      xx: "1.0 + 1.0e-3 * t"
    stress:
      yy: "0.0"
      zz: "0.0"
      xy: "0.0"
      xz: "0.0"
      yz: "0.0"

The condition eliminates the displacements of the nodes of the second sets:

  • The nodes of the two sets of a pair are matched by position, within 10⁻⁶ of the diameter of the mesh. A node without an image aborts the run, so the mesh must conform across the periodic faces.
  • The node common to the first sets of all pairs, the lower corner of the cell, is the origin O and is held fixed, which removes the rigid translation. With one or two pairs these nodes form a face or an edge, and the origin is the lowest of them by x, then y, then z, with coordinates that differ by less than the matching tolerance taken as equal. It keeps zero displacement unless a Dirichlet condition prescribes it. Its image C across each pair, at a distance L along the axis of the pair, carries the column of F̄ along that axis: u(C) − u(O) = (F̄ − I) L eₖ. A prescribed component of F̄ prescribes the displacement of C. A stress-controlled component is a force V P̄ᵢⱼ / L on C, with V the volume of the cell.
  • Every other node of the second sets follows its image on the first sets. A node at an edge or a corner of the cell follows the image that is on the first sets of all its pairs. The Newton residual and tangent are condensed onto the remaining unknowns.
  • At every step the increment of the prescribed components of F̄ is applied to the whole cell, with the increment ΔuO of the displacement of the origin when a Dirichlet condition moves it: u ← u + ΔuO + ΔF̄ (X − X_O). The first Newton iterate is the previous solution plus a translation and a homogeneous deformation, which is exact for a homogeneous material, so the increment does not fall on the elements next to the faces as it would with Dirichlet conditions. Without initial equilibrium, the cell starts in the same way from the affine field of F̄ at the initial time. The increment is the prediction of the step, so the relative tolerance of the solve is measured from the residual without it, as for a predictor of the time integrator. A step that a linear or tangent predictor predicts takes the predicted field instead: on the condensed system, the predictor moves the independent nodes with the corners. A step it does not predict, such as the first step of linear, takes the increment of F̄.

F̄ and P̄ are written to the Exodus output as global variables F_xx, …, F_zz and P_xx, …, P_zz. In Julia they are Norma.periodic_deformation_gradient(model) and Norma.periodic_stress(model). The columns of F̄ along the periodic axes are those the corners carry. With one or two pairs, the other columns, which the condition does not control, are the volume average of F computed over the elements. P̄ is computed as (1/V) Σₐ fₐ ⊗ Xₐ, summed over the internal forces of the material at all nodes, without the forces of Robin and penalty Surface conditions. Because the shape functions interpolate X exactly, this sum is (1/V) ∫ P dV of the finite element field.

The condition requires the quasi static time integrator with the Hessian minimizer solver, in a single-domain simulation, and does not support matrix output (the solve uses the stiffness condensed onto the independent degrees of freedom). A model has at most one Periodic entry, with up to three pairs. Dirichlet conditions may hold the origin and the nodes of the first sets that are on no second set, but not the nodes of the second sets or the corners C, which the periodic condition determines. With two or three pairs, a first set also holds its edges on the second sets of the other pairs (nsx- of a cell periodic along x, y and z holds nodes of nsy+ and nsz+), so a Dirichlet condition on a whole first set aborts, and the message lists the nodes it would prescribe. A node set of the origin, such as nsx-y-z-, or of nodes inside a face, can be held. With one or two pairs, the faces normal to the other axes take other conditions, and only the columns of F̄ along the periodic axes are controlled.

The MicroStructPy converter examples/crystal-plasticity/polycrystal/microstructpy_to_exodus.jl checks that a periodic mesh conforms across its periodic faces. It writes the face node sets nsx-, …, nsz+ (for a periodic axis, the i-th node of nsx+ is the image of the i-th node of nsx-) and a node set for each corner of the box, nsx-y-z- to nsx+y+z+.
